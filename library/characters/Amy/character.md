# Amy

- **Role:** The amygdala (fear centre) who slams the PANIC button and turns the brain room red.
- **Video:** 04 "The Scientist's Insane Time Test" (analysis/videos/04_the_scientists_insane_time_test)
- **Personality:** Jumpy alarm-raiser.

## Design spec

| Part | Spec |
|---|---|
| Body/head | Almond/teardrop seed shape, point down, about 2:3 width:height. Brown #be8661, light side #e3b17b, vertical wood-grain lines #654d39, outline #2a2024 |
| Face | Hollow ring eyes with dot pupils on the upper middle, small mouth (wavy / "o" / grid teeth). Pale-blue sweat drop |
| Limbs | Black noodle arms and legs with ball hands |
| Scale | Tiny: about 1/10 frame height. Stands on the desk next to the PANIC box |

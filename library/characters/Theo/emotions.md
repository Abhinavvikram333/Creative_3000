# Theo — emotions (video 02)

Face-only crops are in this folder. Every expression is a **hard swap** with no in-between drawing. A 1–2-frame transition face (half-lid, one eye closed, or slit eyes) often sits between two holds.

## How the faces are built
- Eyes: solid dot · hollow circle with a dot · flat line · up-arc · half-lid dot · realistic iris (one-off).
- Brows: short thick tapered strokes; their angle carries most of the emotion.
- Mouth: dash · side smile · wavy ~ · toothy grin (black shape with white teeth) · small o · down-turned.
- Extras: sweat drop; vibration arcs beside the head; underwater caustic tint.

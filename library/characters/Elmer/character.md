# Elmer

- **Role:** Bald older volunteer (one of the four test subjects).
- **Video:** 04 "The Scientist's Insane Time Test" (analysis/videos/04_the_scientists_insane_time_test)
- **Personality:** Cheerful old-timer; brags that the fall took forever.

## Design spec
| Part | Spec |
|---|---|
| Head | Round off-white bald head, two fluffy white side tufts |
| Torso | Bean torso in a green harness vest (≈#4e9c5a) with orange straps (#ec9928) and grey buckles; has his own wrist display |
| Limbs | Black noodle |
| Scale | Same body type as Dex, slightly shorter |

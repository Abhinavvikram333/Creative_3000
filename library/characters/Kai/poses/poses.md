# Kai — poses

One crop per distinct body pose (`pose_<id>_<name>_f<frame>.jpg`). Frame ranges inclusive. The character is a rigid
cut-out puppet: the head is one layer, the torso + shirt another, and the limbs are stroked noodle paths. Most acting happens in the face layer.
Poses change by quick 3–6 frame tweens or by being swapped under a dissolve.

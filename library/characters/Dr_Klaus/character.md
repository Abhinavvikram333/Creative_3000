# Dr. Klaus

- **Role:** The scientist ("DR. K" on the blueprint) who drops volunteers off the tower to test whether time slows down.
- **Video:** 04 "The Scientist's Insane Time Test" (analysis/videos/04_the_scientists_insane_time_test)
- **Personality:** Calm, deadpan, quietly delighted mad scientist. Pushes people off towers with a straight face and winks at the camera.

## Design spec

| Part | Spec |
|---|---|
| Head | Perfect circle, warm off-white #f3eeed, outline #2a2024 ~6–8 px at 720p |
| Hair | Black (#2a2024) glossy **bowl cut** with a centre part line, like a helmet over the top ~35 % of the head |
| Glasses | Big **round black wire glasses**, about 1/4 head width each, joined by a bridge. Lenses sometimes catch a pale blue sky tint |
| Eyes | Small black dots inside the lenses. Heavy half-lids for smug. One lens closed (line) for the wink |
| Moustache | Small black bushy "toothbrush" moustache under the glasses. **No mouth drawn** in most frames |
| Torso | Long white **lab coat** (#e6eaee, shade #c9ced6), A-line to the knees, lapels, breast pocket with a red and a blue pen, thin dark-red tie |
| Limbs | Black noodle arms with ball hands, thin dark trouser legs |
| Proportions | About 4 head-heights, same scale as Dex |
| Props | CHRONO-2 (fastening it), giant silver stopwatch reading "2.49", clipboard with checklist and red "0/4" |

## 3. Visual-support techniques used
- **Literal illustration**: reels landing on the spoken words (beats 1, 2, 4), $100 bill insert, CREDITS counter.
- **Reaction close-up**: dutch CU with face flicker (beat 4); final deadpan MS (beat 7).
- **Unusual POV gag**: camera inside the machine (beat 3).
- **Cutaway diagram / explainer prop**: par-sheet strip, glint + x1 badge, stamp + tally counter, strip→wheel morph,
  X-ray wireframe (beats 6–7).
- **Running gag / motif**: reel 3 stops one click short in four separate shots (#91–120, ≈#236, #455–470, #875+), and
  the progressive LED keeps ticking in the background.

# Script — 01 "This Tree Hates You"

## 1. Narration (SRT cleaned)
> If you ever sit under this tree, it can kill you. Imagine you're stranded on a beach and it's the only tree in sight.
> It's called the manchineel. It looks completely harmless, with little green apples hanging from the branches, but
> there's a big red X painted on its trunk, and you're about to find out why.
>
> The first night, a storm rolls in. Naturally, you run under the tree for cover. Big mistake. You see, every leaf on
> this tree is full of a milky sap, and the rain carries it straight onto your skin. Within minutes, you're covered in
> blisters. This sap is so strong it can strip the paint off a car. Lesson learned.
>
> By the next morning, though, you're starving, and those little apples smell incredible. One bite actually tastes
> sweet for about a second. Then your mouth starts burning, and it feels like you swallowed fire.
>
> At this point, nature calls. The only thing around to use is a handful of big soft leaves. Yeah. Same sap, same
> blisters, just in a much worse place.
>
> When night falls, you're freezing. Fortunately, you have plenty of wood. Unfortunately, it's from the same tree. The
> second it catches, the smoke turns toxic. It burns your eyes until you can't see a thing, and every breath scorches
> your lungs.
>
> So, if you're ever stranded on a beach, maybe just sit in the sand.

SRT fixes:
- "When nightfall is" → **"When night falls,"**. The on-screen captions read "When" / "night" / "falls,".
- "[music]" tag removed.
- The on-screen captions write "red x" in lowercase.

  fire.
- **Running motif**: the red X (trunk → logs) and the apple (hanging → bitten → rolling in at the end).

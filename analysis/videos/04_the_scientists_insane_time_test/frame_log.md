# Frame log: 04 "The Scientist's Insane Time Test"

720x1280, 30 fps, 1200 frames (40.0 s). Frame numbers are 1-based (f1 = 0.000 s, t = (n-1)/30).
Method: I opened all 40 per-second grids (sec_000 to sec_039, 30 numbered frames each). Where a grid was too small to read faces, LED digits or text, I opened full-res frames and cropped strips of them.
Characters: **Dr. Klaus** (scientist), **Dex** (volunteer), **Noggin** (brain). Extra characters (my names): **Elmer** (bald volunteer), **Gil** (purple-shirt volunteer), **Tad** (bowl-cut volunteer), **Amy** (amygdala), support group **Gus / Carl / Deb / Ray / Mo** (these five names are printed on their name tags in the video), BBQ friends **Cooper**, **Hank** and **Biscuit** (dog), and the **Digit Gang** (the six LED digits "418073").
Caption = the word card on screen (white bold sans, black outline, centred at y≈900).

# Script — 02 "Your Brain's Secret Idea Zone"

## 1. Full narration (cleaned)

> If you fall asleep holding two steel balls, you might wake up a genius. You see, first you put two metal plates on the floor, sit down right above them, and hold a steel ball in each hand. Then you just let yourself drift off. Now, here's the weird part. The second you fall asleep, your hands go limp, the balls smash into the plates, and the noise jolts you awake. Sounds like the worst nap ever. But Thomas Edison was rumored to do it on purpose. That's because right before deep sleep, your brain hits a strange halfway state called hypnagogia, where it starts connecting ideas it never would while you're awake. Normally, you sink right past it and forget everything. But the crash yanks you out right in the middle of it. So you wake up holding the idea instead of the balls.

Notes:
- `2/SRT.txt` stops at "forget everything." (32.4 s). The last two sentences ("But the crash yanks you out right in the middle of it. So you wake up holding the idea instead of the balls.") are recovered from the on-screen word captions (#1011–1172).
- The SRT spells the term "hypnogogia"; the on-screen caption and the title card spell it **"hypnagogia"**. The title card is the spelling to use.
